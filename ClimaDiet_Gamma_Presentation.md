# ClimaDiet: Climate-Aware AI Clinical Nutrition
Eating right for your body, your region, and your environment.

---

# The Problem: Why Generic Diets Fail
- **Climate Blindspots:** Standard diets ignore local weather (e.g., suggesting heavy, heat-generating meals during a 40°C summer heatwave).
- **Clinical Danger:** Most AI diet apps hallucinate macros and fail to safely manage strict clinical constraints like diabetes, GERD, or severe allergies.
- **Impracticality:** Diet plans often demand expensive, imported ingredients rather than utilizing affordable, local, pantry-ready items.

---

# The Solution: What is ClimaDiet?
- **Hyper-Personalized:** Tailors exact macro-nutrient targets based on your unique BMI, BMR, and health goals.
- **Climate-Synced:** Integrates with real-time weather APIs to recommend cooling foods in summer and warming, immune-boosting foods in winter.
- **Clinically Safe:** Enforces hard dietary restrictions (e.g., Halal, Vegan, allergen-free) with absolute mathematical certainty.

---

# How It Works: The Hybrid AI Architecture
- **Phase 1: Clinical LLM Classifier:** A fast AI layer extracts medical conditions, allergies, and strict rules from user input.
- **Phase 2: Live Weather Sync:** Fetches regional 7-day forecasts to apply climate modifiers to the diet template.
- **Phase 3: Deterministic Math Engine:** Uses strict algorithms (not AI guesses) to match verified database meals to the user's exact macro targets.

---

# Usage & Practicality
- **Standard Mode:** Generates complete 7-day meal plans optimized for your exact budget and location.
- **Sickness & Recovery Mode:** Instantly pivots to a health-first 3-day plan, providing targeted "Must Eat" and "Strictly Avoid" clinical advice.
- **Restaurant Integration:** Automatically maps recommended meals to local restaurants for users on the go.

---

# Core Advantages & Efficiency
- **Zero Hallucinations:** Math-based macro matching guarantees 100% nutritional accuracy.
- **Cost-Efficient:** Leverages existing local pantry items to drastically reduce grocery bills.
- **Lightning Fast:** By removing the LLM from the heavy math, plan generation is instantaneous and resource-efficient.
